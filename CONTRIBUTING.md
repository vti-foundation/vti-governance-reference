# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the VTI Deterministic Governance Reference repository.

This repository is maintained by VTI Foundation Inc. as a limited public reference surface for implementation, interoperability, and technical evaluation.

## Contribution Scope

Contributions may be appropriate where they improve:

* public reference documentation;
* implementation-neutral schemas;
* examples;
* validation utilities;
* interoperability guidance;
* infrastructure adapters;
* tests for publicly released interfaces; or
* developer usability.

Contributions should remain within the scope of the public reference repository.

## Do Not Submit

Please do not submit:

* confidential information;
* unpublished VTI standards text;
* proprietary VTI implementation methods;
* certification procedures or internal conformity-assessment materials;
* unpublished patent claims or claim-oriented implementation details;
* material subject to nondisclosure obligations;
* proprietary third-party source code;
* trade secrets;
* personal or sensitive information; or
* content you do not have the right to publish.

If you are unsure whether material is appropriate for public contribution, do not submit it through a public issue or pull request.

## Standards Development

This repository is not the standards-development venue for VTI Foundation Inc.

A pull request or issue does not amend, interpret, supersede, or establish requirements for any VTI standard, specification, certification program, or conformity-assessment process.

Technical discussion in this repository should therefore be understood as implementation-oriented and non-normative unless expressly stated otherwise.

## Issues

Issues may be used to report:

* documentation errors;
* schema inconsistencies;
* validation problems;
* interoperability questions;
* implementation defects in publicly released code; or
* other matters directly related to repository contents.

Do not use public issues to report security vulnerabilities.

See `SECURITY.md` for security reporting guidance.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests should:

* address a clearly defined issue or improvement;
* remain narrowly scoped;
* avoid unrelated formatting or structural changes;
* include documentation where behavior changes;
* include tests where applicable; and
* accurately describe what has and has not been implemented.

Do not describe experimental or incomplete work as production-ready.

## Licensing of Contributions

By submitting a contribution to this repository, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution and that it may be distributed under the license applicable to this repository.

Contributors should not submit material subject to incompatible licensing terms.

## Intellectual Property

Submission of a contribution does not grant rights to:

* separately maintained VTI standards;
* VTI certification programs;
* VTI trademarks;
* unpublished technical materials;
* patent rights outside the scope, if any, of the applicable repository license; or
* other intellectual property not contained in the submitted contribution.

See `NOTICE.md` for additional information.

## Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to engage professionally, focus on technical substance, and avoid abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive behavior.

VTI Foundation Inc. reserves the right to moderate participation in order to maintain a constructive technical environment.

## Questions

Questions about contribution scope may be raised through an appropriate repository issue unless the question involves confidential, security-sensitive, or unpublished material.

For matters involving standards, certification, trademarks, licensing, or other protected intellectual property, use official VTI Foundation Inc. contact channels.
